M. von WedelstaedtColoring books and puzzle books for kids

I have three kids, and in the afternoon I was looking for five quiet minutes. Coloring books were supposed to help, but most were either too babyish or too complicated, and plenty of them I just didn't like…

So I started making my own. First for the little ones, with thick lines, big pictures, one picture per page. Then something for my oldest, who was already annoyed because she wanted her own…
When my three are bent over their pictures, it's all worth it. What began as a first book for home quickly turned into a whole series, and more are coming. At the same time the first books for my oldest came along. The way it looks, more will keep joining…
